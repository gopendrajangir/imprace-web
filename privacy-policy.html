Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Imprace ("the app", "we", "us") is an AI interviewer that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles and, just as importantly, what it never collects.

Everything runs on-device. The AI model, speech recognition, and feedback all execute locally on your phone. Your interview audio, transcripts, and responses are not uploaded to us or to any third party.

1. Data we do not collect

Because Imprace processes interviews locally, we do not collect, transmit, or store on our servers:

  • Your interview audio or voice recordings
  • Your spoken or typed answers, or the transcripts of them
  • The questions generated or the feedback and scores produced during a session
  • Any account credentials — the app does not require you to create an account

2. Data stored on your device

To make the app work, some information is saved locally in the app's private storage on your device. This stays on your device and is under your control:

  • Downloaded AI models — the on-device model files the app needs to run offline.
  • Session data — your practice history, feedback, and settings, if you choose to save them.
  • App preferences — such as your selected interview type or language.

You can remove this data at any time (see Deleting your data).

3. Permissions the app requests

  • Microphone — used to capture your spoken answers so the app can transcribe and evaluate them on-device. Audio is processed locally and is not sent anywhere.
  • Storage — used to save downloaded models and your session data on the device.
  • Network access — used only to download AI models and app updates. Describe any other network use, or state "The app makes no other network requests."

When you download the app, the app store (Google Play / Apple App Store) processes your download according to its own privacy policy, which we do not control.

6. Children's privacy

Imprace is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Deleting your data

Since your data lives on your device, you control it directly. You can:

  • Delete individual sessions from within the app
  • Clear the app's data from your device's system settings
  • Uninstall the app, which removes all locally stored data and downloaded models

8. Security

Data stored by Imprace resides in the app's private storage area, protected by your device's operating system. Keeping your device secure with a passcode or biometric lock, and keeping the OS up to date, is the best way to protect this data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the app.
