Frequently asked questions

Does my interview data leave my device?

No. Imprace runs the AI model, speech processing, and feedback entirely on your device. Your audio, answers, and results are not uploaded to us or any third party. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why does the app need microphone access?

The microphone captures your spoken answers so the app can transcribe and evaluate them locally. Nothing is sent off the device. You can revoke the permission any time in your device settings, though the app needs it for voice-based interviews.

Why is the first launch downloading something?

Imprace downloads the on-device AI model the first time so it can then run fully offline. This uses roughly X hundred MB / GB of storage and is a one-time download (plus occasional updates).

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual sessions in the app, clear the app's data from your device settings, or uninstall the app to remove everything, including downloaded models.

The app is running slowly or a session failed. What can I try?

  • Make sure your device has enough free storage and battery.
  • Close other heavy apps so the model has room to run.
  • Restart the app, and if needed, your device.
  • Confirm you're on the latest version from the app store.
  • If a model download failed, retry on a stable connection.
